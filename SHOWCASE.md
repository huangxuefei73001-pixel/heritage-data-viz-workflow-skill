## What changes between samples

- the **visual question**;
- the **evidence encoding**;
- the **surface mode**;
- the amount of supporting metadata.

## What remains stable

- evidence before decoration;
- one dominant visual object;
- explicit reading logic;
- stable category semantics;
- source / provenance awareness;
- deterministic, inspectable output.

See [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) for the complete workflow and [`references/reference-project.md`](references/reference-project.md) for the Observation Report series from which the method was distilled.
